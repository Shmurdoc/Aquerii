# Legal Documents & Templates

Include templates and required legal artefacts for launch.

- **Terms of Service**: user-facing TOS, including permitted use, IP ownership, limitation of liability.
- **Privacy Policy**: clear description of data collected, retention, sharing, and user controls.
- **DPA**: data processing terms for enterprise customers.
- **Acceptable Use Policy**: prohibited activities and enforcement process.
- **MSA / Order Form**: templates for commercial customers (pricing, termination, support levels).
- **Intellectual Property**: contributor terms and open-source usage policy.
