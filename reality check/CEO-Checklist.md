# CEO Checklist — Launch Readiness

Purpose: decisions, governance, finance, go/no-go criteria.

- **Business Model**: validated pricing, ARR targets, cost model, CAC assumptions.
- **Go/No-Go Criteria**: clear KPIs (reliability, security, NPS, MRR, time-to-recover) and thresholds for launch.
- **Executive Owners**: assign Product, Engineering, Security, Legal, Compliance, Sales, Support leads.
- **Funding & Runway**: confirm budget for 12 months post-launch including cloud, people, third-party services.
- **Customer SLAs & Refund Policy**: approved SLA, uptime commitments, credit/refund process.
- **Contracts & Sales Docs**: standard Master Services Agreement (MSA), Data Processing Agreement (DPA), reseller terms.
- **Insurance**: cyber insurance reviewed; limits acceptable to stakeholders.
- **Regulatory Risk Review**: privacy/regulation mapping for target markets (GDPR, CCPA, sector-specific).
- **Board Communication Plan**: board materials and launch announcement timing.
