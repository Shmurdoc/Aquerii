# Launch Plan & Roadmap

- **Pre-Launch (T-8 to T-2 weeks)**:
  - Finalize go/no-go criteria and sign-offs.
  - Run full load and security tests; complete any remediation.
  - Prepare marketing & support collateral and customer onboarding flows.
  - Legal sign-off on TOS, Privacy Policy, and DPA.

- **Launch Week**:
  - Staged rollout (internal → beta customers → public) with canary monitoring.
  - 24/7 on-call coverage and war-room for first 72 hours.
  - Real-time dashboards and customer communications channels active.

- **Post-Launch (0–12 weeks)**:
  - Weekly stability reviews, SLA adherence checks, customer feedback loops.
  - Prioritized backlog for P1/P2 fixes discovered in first month.
  - Business metrics monitoring (MRR, churn, NPS) and adjustment of go-to-market.
