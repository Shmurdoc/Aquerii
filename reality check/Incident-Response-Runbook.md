# Incident Response Runbook

- **On-Call Roster**: primary, secondary, contact methods, escalation ladders.
- **Severity Definitions**: S0 (complete outage) → S4 (minor issue) with response SLAs and owners.
- **First Actions**: identify blast radius, enable mitigations (traffic shifting, feature flags), communicate internally.
- **Customer Communication**: templates for status pages, customer emails, and post-incident reports.
- **Root Cause Analysis**: timeline, contributing factors, remediation tasks, and verification steps.
- **Postmortem Process**: blameless postmortems, action item tracking, measurement of recurrence.
