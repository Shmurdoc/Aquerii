# Monitoring & Observability

- **Key Metrics**: latency, error rates, throughput, CPU/RAM, queue lengths, model quality metrics.
- **Dashboards**: SLO-focused dashboards with burn rate and month-to-date views.
- **Alerting**: documented alert rules, noise-reduction, pager escalation, runbooks linked to alerts.
- **Logging**: structured logs, correlation IDs, sensitive data redaction rules.
- **Tracing**: distributed tracing for service flows and slow-path identification.
- **Synthetic Monitoring**: periodic synthetic transactions to detect regressions before users.
