# Data Privacy & Protection

- **Data Inventory**: catalog personal data, processing purposes, retention windows, and owners.
- **DPA Template**: include roles, subprocessors, security measures, breach notification timelines.
- **Consent & Notices**: UX flows for collecting consent, data subject rights handling (access, deletion, portability).
- **Minimization & Retention**: only store required fields; retention schedule and automated purging.
- **Cross-Border Data Flows**: model transfer mechanisms (SCCs, adequacy), mapping of EU/UK residents.
- **Breach Notification**: internal escalation, regulator notification windows, customer notification templates.
