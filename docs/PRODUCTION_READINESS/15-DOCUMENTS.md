# DOCUMENTS - REAL-WORLD READINESS REVIEW

## Verdict
Documents are useful only if they support versioning, approvals, signatures, search, retention, and metadata. A file list is not a document management system.

## What Exists Today
- Notes and file-oriented pages.
- Basic document navigation.
- Limited evidence of control or collaboration depth.

## What Is Missing
- Version history and rollback.
- E-signature workflows.
- Document templates and controlled variables.
- Metadata, tags, and full-text search.
- Retention policies and access controls.
- Review and approval routing.
- OCR and attachment intelligence.

## Why It Fails in Real Companies
- Contracts, policies, proposals, and internal procedures require control.
- If versions are loose, the wrong file gets used.
- If approval and signature are missing, legal and finance will not trust the system.

## Real-World Requirements
- Document states such as draft, review, approved, signed, archived.
- Shared links with permissions and expiry.
- Audit history for every change.
- Linkage to CRM, sales, support, and HR records.
- Searchable document libraries by business context.

## Templates Needed
- Proposals.
- Contracts.
- NDAs.
- Statements of work.
- Internal policy documents.
- Meeting notes and decision records.

## Automation Ideas
- Route documents for approval when they are created.
- Trigger e-signature requests on approved contracts.
- Archive documents after expiration or close.
- Create tasks from document comments.
- Pull document data into reports and dashboards.

## Fix Strategy
1. Add versioning, permissions, and approval states.
2. Add templates and variable insertion.
3. Add e-signature and retention controls.
4. Add OCR and search indexing.
5. Tie documents to every core business workflow.

## Done Means
- Teams can manage critical documents without spreadsheet chaos.
- Legal and finance can trust the workflow.
- Every important file has a traceable life cycle.
