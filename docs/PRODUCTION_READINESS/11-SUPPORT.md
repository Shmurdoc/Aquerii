# SUPPORT - REAL-WORLD READINESS REVIEW

## Verdict
Support is not ready for serious customer operations. A real support system needs tickets, SLAs, knowledge base, macros, assignment logic, and customer visibility.

## What Exists Today
- Support pages and ticket-oriented surfaces.
- Knowledge base concepts.
- Basic service desk framing.

## What Is Missing
- True ticket lifecycle with states, priorities, and escalations.
- SLA timers and breach handling.
- Customer portal access.
- Macros, canned replies, and response templates.
- Omnichannel intake from email, chat, and forms.
- CSAT and customer satisfaction tracking.
- Internal notes, merges, duplicates, and incident handling.

## Why It Fails in Real Companies
- Support is a retention engine.
- If tickets are not owned, tracked, and escalated, customers feel ignored.
- Without knowledge management, support gets slower instead of better.

## Real-World Requirements
- Customer-linked ticket history.
- SLA and escalation policies by segment or plan.
- Agent workload and queue management.
- Major incident and root-cause workflows.
- Reporting on first response, resolution time, and satisfaction.

## Templates Needed
- Support reply macros.
- Escalation templates.
- Knowledge base article templates.
- Incident response templates.
- CSAT follow-up templates.

## Automation Ideas
- Assign tickets by skill or priority.
- Escalate SLA breaches.
- Suggest knowledge base articles before response.
- Auto-close resolved tickets after delay.
- Trigger CSAT surveys after resolution.
- Create bug reports from repeated customer complaints.

## Fix Strategy
1. Build the real ticket lifecycle.
2. Add omnichannel intake and customer context.
3. Add SLA, macros, and escalation logic.
4. Add KB and CSAT flows.
5. Add support dashboards for leadership.

## Done Means
- Customers can get help without chaos.
- Agents can work from one queue.
- Leadership can measure service quality.
