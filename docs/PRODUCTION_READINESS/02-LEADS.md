# LEADS - REAL-WORLD READINESS REVIEW

## Verdict
Lead management is far too shallow. A business cannot rely on a lead system that only captures basic records and assigns them loosely. Leads need routing, scoring, enrichment, compliance, and nurture.

## What Exists Today
- Basic lead capture and editing.
- Simple scoring concepts.
- Basic linkage to CRM records.
- Limited UX around assignment and follow-up.

## What Is Missing
- Web forms, landing pages, API capture, imports, and event-based lead intake.
- Duplicate detection and suppression across lead, contact, and company records.
- Consent tracking and regional compliance rules.
- Qualification stages that reflect real sales processes.
- Lead source, campaign attribution, and lifecycle stage history.
- Territory, language, product, and segment-based routing.
- Speed-to-lead SLAs and escalation rules.
- Nurture paths and re-engagement logic.

## Why It Fails in Real Companies
- A lead is worthless if nobody follows up at the right time with the right context.
- Without scoring and routing, sales teams waste time on poor-fit leads while good leads age out.
- Without compliance and suppression, marketing can create legal and reputation risk.

## Real-World Requirements
- Unified intake from forms, chat, imports, APIs, and integrations.
- Automatic lead enrichment and normalization.
- Clear qualification fields and disqualification reasons.
- Conversion to contact, account, and deal with traceable lineage.
- Assignment policies by segment, geography, product, or capacity.
- SLA timers, reminders, and escalation paths.

## Templates Needed
- Lead capture forms by campaign type.
- Qualification checklists.
- SDR call and follow-up templates.
- Nurture sequence templates.
- Lead scoring templates by product or market.

## Automation Ideas
- Score leads from source, behavior, and firmographics.
- Route leads to the right rep instantly.
- Create tasks when a lead is untouched for too long.
- Start nurture flows for unready leads.
- Suppress leads after unsubscribe or compliance flags.
- Convert accepted leads to CRM records automatically.

## Fix Strategy
1. Build robust intake and normalization.
2. Add routing, scoring, and SLA enforcement.
3. Add consent and suppression controls.
4. Add nurture sequences and re-engagement rules.
5. Add lead analytics by source, rep, and segment.

## Done Means
- Every lead has a source, score, owner, and next action.
- No qualified lead falls through the cracks.
- Marketing and sales can trust the lead pipeline.
