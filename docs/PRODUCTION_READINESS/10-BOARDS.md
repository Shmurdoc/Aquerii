# BOARDS - REAL-WORLD READINESS REVIEW

## Verdict
The boards module looks like a task board, but businesses need project delivery control. That means dependencies, subtasks, templates, automations, timelines, and reporting.

## What Exists Today
- Boards, cards, and drag-and-drop behavior.
- Basic task tracking concepts.
- Limited sign that the module supports serious project governance.

## What Is Missing
- Subtasks and nested work breakdown.
- Dependencies and blockers.
- Gantt or timeline planning.
- Recurring tasks and task templates.
- Work-in-progress limits and swimlanes.
- Role-based board views.
- Time tracking, workload, and capacity planning.
- Status automation tied to due dates and approvals.

## Why It Fails in Real Companies
- Teams do not manage real projects with only cards in columns.
- Delivery fails when dependencies and owners are not explicit.
- Management needs burnup, throughput, and risk visibility.

## Real-World Requirements
- Project templates by function.
- Clear ownership, due dates, and dependency maps.
- Board-level and task-level permissions.
- Reporting on cycle time, backlog health, and overdue work.
- Integration with calendar, docs, support, and automation.

## Templates Needed
- Project templates.
- Onboarding boards.
- Content production boards.
- Bug triage boards.
- Launch checklists.

## Automation Ideas
- Move cards when a dependency clears.
- Create subtasks from a project template.
- Escalate overdue work.
- Assign tasks based on workload.
- Notify stakeholders when milestones slip.
- Trigger reminders from due dates or status changes.

## Fix Strategy
1. Add subtasks, dependencies, and timeline view.
2. Add templates and recurring structures.
3. Add workload and governance controls.
4. Add board analytics and alerts.
5. Link boards to automation and reports.

## Done Means
- Teams can run actual projects and not just move cards.
- Managers can see blockers and delivery risk.
- Boards become a delivery system, not a visual toy.
