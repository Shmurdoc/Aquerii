# CALENDAR - REAL-WORLD READINESS REVIEW

## Verdict
Calendar support is not production ready. Real calendar use means sync, availability, scheduling, conflicts, reminders, and resource management across providers.

## What Exists Today
- Calendar pages and meeting concepts.
- Minimal scheduling flow.
- No proof of full external provider integration.

## What Is Missing
- Google Calendar and Outlook sync.
- Free/busy availability checks.
- Event conflict detection.
- Time zone handling that survives global teams.
- Meeting links and location support.
- Resource calendars for rooms, equipment, and staff.
- Scheduling pages and booking links.
- Reminders, follow-up tasks, and meeting notes.

## Why It Fails in Real Companies
- Without provider sync, meetings drift away from the system.
- Double bookings and time zone mistakes break trust immediately.
- A calendar that does not respect availability is just a note-taking tool.

## Real-World Requirements
- Two-way sync with major calendar providers.
- Host, attendee, and resource availability.
- Reschedule and cancellation flows.
- Meeting type templates and booking policies.
- Audit trail for invitations and changes.

## Templates Needed
- Meeting templates.
- Agenda templates.
- Reminder templates.
- Scheduling page templates.
- Resource booking templates.

## Automation Ideas
- Create a follow-up task after a meeting ends.
- Block out travel or focus time automatically.
- Route booking requests based on team availability.
- Send reminders before the meeting.
- Create a summary note or task checklist after the event.
- Escalate conflicts when a key meeting is double-booked.

## Fix Strategy
1. Add provider sync and availability logic.
2. Add resource and meeting type templates.
3. Add conflict and reschedule handling.
4. Add task follow-up automation.
5. Surface calendar data in CRM, support, and dashboard views.

## Done Means
- Users can schedule without leaving the system.
- Meetings stay synced with external calendars.
- The calendar becomes a dependable operational layer.
