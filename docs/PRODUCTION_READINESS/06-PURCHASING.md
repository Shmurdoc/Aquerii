# PURCHASING - REAL-WORLD READINESS REVIEW

## Verdict
Purchasing is not ready for businesses that need supplier control, approval chains, and audit-safe procurement. A real purchasing module has to manage sourcing, purchase orders, receipts, exceptions, and payment handoff.

## What Exists Today
- Basic purchasing surface area.
- Simple procurement-style pages.
- No mature source-to-pay discipline.

## What Is Missing
- Supplier onboarding and vendor master data.
- Request for quote and vendor comparison workflows.
- Purchase order approval by amount, category, and budget.
- Receipt matching and three-way match.
- Partial delivery and backorder handling.
- Supplier terms, lead times, and contract tracking.
- Spend controls and budget enforcement.

## Why It Fails in Real Companies
- Procurement errors become cost leakage, stockouts, and accounting cleanup.
- Without approvals, anyone can create spend with no guardrails.
- Without receipt matching, finance cannot trust liabilities or inventory handoff.

## Real-World Requirements
- Supplier profiles with tax, banking, and compliance data.
- RFQ and quote comparison support.
- PO lifecycle states from draft to approved to received.
- Goods received notes and discrepancy handling.
- Budget checks and exception routing.

## Templates Needed
- Purchase order templates.
- RFQ templates.
- Supplier onboarding forms.
- Receiving checklists.
- Three-way match exception forms.

## Automation Ideas
- Generate POs from reorder thresholds.
- Route POs for approval by amount or category.
- Alert on overdue supplier acknowledgements.
- Match receipts against POs automatically.
- Escalate invoice exceptions to finance.
- Create replenishment tasks when stock is low.

## Fix Strategy
1. Build supplier master data and approvals.
2. Add PO lifecycle and receipt tracking.
3. Add three-way match and exception handling.
4. Add spending controls and budget checks.
5. Connect purchasing to inventory and accounting.

## Done Means
- Procurement can control spend and prove what was ordered.
- Receiving and finance can reconcile without manual detective work.
- Supplier performance is measurable.
