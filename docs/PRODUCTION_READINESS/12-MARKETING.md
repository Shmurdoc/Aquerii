# MARKETING - REAL-WORLD READINESS REVIEW

## Verdict
Marketing is not production ready because it does not yet behave like a real demand generation system. Real marketing needs segmentation, journeys, forms, landing pages, consent, and reporting.

## What Exists Today
- Campaign, segment, and email template pages.
- Basic marketing surfaces.
- Limited evidence of a true campaign execution engine.

## What Is Missing
- Campaigns that actually send with reliable tracking.
- Segmentation by behavior, lifecycle, and firmographic data.
- Landing pages and forms.
- Consent and suppression logic.
- Drip journeys and branching.
- A/B testing and attribution.
- Lead handoff to CRM and sales.
- Reporting on pipeline impact, not only opens and clicks.

## Why It Fails in Real Companies
- Marketing is not about sending messages. It is about creating qualified pipeline.
- Without segmentation and compliance, campaigns become noise or risk.
- Without attribution, leaders cannot tell what worked.

## Real-World Requirements
- Lifecycle-aware audience management.
- Consent-aware messaging.
- Lead capture connected to CRM and email.
- Landing page and form builders.
- Campaign analytics tied to revenue.

## Templates Needed
- Campaign briefs.
- Landing page templates.
- Drip journey templates.
- Form templates.
- A/B test templates.

## Automation Ideas
- Enroll leads in journeys based on behavior.
- Pause campaigns when consent is withdrawn.
- Move contacts between segments automatically.
- Trigger sales alerts on high-intent actions.
- Generate campaign performance reports on schedule.
- Suppress contacts after bounce or unsubscribe.

## Fix Strategy
1. Add true campaign delivery and tracking.
2. Add segmentation and consent rules.
3. Add landing pages and forms.
4. Add attribution and reporting.
5. Tie marketing outcomes to CRM and sales.

## Done Means
- Marketing can create demand and prove impact.
- Campaigns behave like business processes, not mail merges.
- Sales can trust the handoff from marketing.
